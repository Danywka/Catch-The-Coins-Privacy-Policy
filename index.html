What information does the Application obtain and how is it used?

The Application utilizes an account system allowing users to sign in (for example, via Google Account integration) to save and synchronize their game progress. When you use the account system, the Application obtains and stores basic account information (such as your display name) and gameplay data, including coins, levels, player colors, customization items, and settings.

This data is stored locally on your device and can be backed up to your personal cloud storage (such as Google Drive) to ensure you don't lose your progress. Don't worry about Google Drive warnings! We use it exclusively to securely store your account game data so your progress stays safe.

The DanywkaGames Hub application does not collect or use any user data.

Does the Application collect precise real time location information of the device?

This Application does not collect precise information about the location of your computer or mobile device.

Your game data and account information are used strictly within the Application to maintain your profile and progress. We do not sell, trade, or otherwise transfer your personal information to outside parties, except as necessary to provide cloud synchronization via your own connected cloud storage (like Google Drive).
